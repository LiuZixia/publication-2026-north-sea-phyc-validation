# Provider Access Request Drafts

Nine ready-to-send drafts. Replace `[YOUR NAME]`, `[YOUR AFFILIATION]`, and `[YOUR EMAIL]` before sending, and confirm the current contact address on each provider's page — addresses change and none is hard-coded here.

**The study does not wait on any of these.** Under `config/access_and_licence_policy.json`, a dataset obtainable only by request is treated as unavailable at the current stage and its consequence is applied immediately. A reply is welcome and re-admits the dataset through the normal Stage 2 route, recorded as a dated addition in the protocol-change register. Record the send date in `metadata/stage1/qualification/provider_access_requests.csv` (`sent_utc`).

Each draft states plainly what is being asked and why. None implies that a refusal blocks the work, because it does not.

## DS08 — AWI Helgoland Roads (licence clarification)

**To:** principal investigator named on PANGAEA `10.1594/PANGAEA.960407` and `10.1594/PANGAEA.862910`
**Subject:** Licence terms for Helgoland Roads phytoplankton carbon and biovolume datasets (PANGAEA)

> Dear Colleagues,
>
> I am preparing a validation study of Copernicus Marine North-West Shelf modelled phytoplankton carbon against independent in-situ observations of North Sea phytoplankton biomass, and the Helgoland Roads time series is the most informative record I have identified for the German Bight.
>
> Working from the PANGAEA series `10.1594/PANGAEA.960407`, I see that the abundance datasets carry CC-BY licences, while the datasets reporting total biomass as carbon and total biovolume render as "Licensing unknown: Please contact principal investigator/authors to gain access and request licensing terms" (for example `10.1594/PANGAEA.862910`).
>
> Could you tell me:
>
> 1. under what terms the carbon- and biovolume-resolved annual datasets may be used in a peer-reviewed validation study;
> 2. whether a moratorium currently applies, and to which years;
> 3. whether a citation and co-authorship or acknowledgement arrangement would be expected.
>
> To be clear about what I am and am not asking: I am not requesting any file be sent. I would like to know the terms, so I can decide whether to use the openly licensed abundance series alone. My analysis is proceeding on that basis in the meantime, so there is no deadline attached to this enquiry.
>
> The study is observational validation only; no Helgoland data would be redistributed, and all products would cite the series and its authors as directed.
